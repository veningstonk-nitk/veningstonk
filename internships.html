Internships

Short-term research internship opportunities with TEAMS Lab (Tiny & Edge AI Machines and Systems), Department of Computer Science and Engineering, NIT Karnataka Surathkal.

Current Availability

No stipend-supported internship positions are available at this time.

Self-funded / unpaid research internships may still be considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to project bandwidth. Interested students should reach out well ahead of the intake windows below, with a CV and a short note on which topic area interests them.

Preferred Internship Windows

Applications are considered primarily for these two intake periods each year.

Winter intake
Dec–Jan
Summer intake
May–Jun

Potential Internship Topics

Indicative areas aligned with ongoing TEAMS Lab work. These are starting points for discussion, not fixed project briefs — a concrete scope is worked out with the intern based on background and interest.

TinyML model compression & quantization

Designing and evaluating sub-10 KB neural networks for on-device signal classification, building on the lab's ECG beat-classification work.

On-device environmental monitoring

Lightweight segmentation/detection models for edge deployment in environmental monitoring tasks such as floating waste detection.

Small language models at the edge

Evaluating and compressing small language models for disaster-response and environmental-intelligence applications on constrained hardware.

Edge AI for agriculture

On-device crop-disease detection and related embedded-AI pipelines for resource-constrained farming settings.

Embedded AI for weather/climate nowcasting

Micro-location-scale nowcasting models suited to embedded deployment for agriculture-protection use cases.

Edge-deployed digital twins

Digital-twin pipelines built around sensor/analyser data, designed for on-device or near-edge deployment.

Verification & validation of edge-deployed AI models

Evaluation protocols for quantized/compressed models — benchmarking accuracy, robustness, and footprint trade-offs across hardware targets.

How to Apply

  • Email a short note on your background and which topic area(s) above interest you, along with your CV, to the contact address on the main contact section.
  • Apply ahead of the Dec–Jan or May–Jun windows; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis as bandwidth allows.
  • Since there is currently no stipend support, please indicate in your note whether you are seeking a self-funded/credit-bearing internship.
