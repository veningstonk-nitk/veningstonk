TEAMS Lab — Tiny & Edge AI Machines and Systems

A departmental research lab working on Edge AI and small/tiny AI models — spanning healthcare, environmental intelligence, and societal applications — with an emphasis on models deployable under tight memory, power, and connectivity constraints.

Current Projects

Edge AI / TinyML-aligned work currently underway. Review this list before publishing — some entries are carried over from the wider research program; keep only what you consider part of TEAMS Lab.

  1. EcoEdgeAI — Dal Lake plastic waste detection

    Lightweight on-device segmentation for floating plastic-waste detection; patent application in finalization alongside a manuscript under conditional acceptance.

    Ongoing

  2. EdgeSLM

    Small language model deployment on edge hardware, proposed for disaster response and environmental-intelligence use cases.

    Proposed

  3. KrishiRakshak

    Crop-disease detection (maize, wheat) for ST farmers on edge hardware; pitched to the TIH-IoT Hub at IIT Bombay, currently addressing panel feedback.

    Ongoing

  4. KESAR-NOW (saffron micro-nowcasting)

    Embedded-AI nowcasting of extreme weather at micro-location scale to protect Kashmir saffron farming; JKST&IC SRE 2026-27 proposal in preparation.

    In preparation

  5. Edge-deployed personal digital twin

    Institute seed grant proposal for a digital twin built around a bioelectrical impedance analyser, deployed at the edge.

    Proposed
