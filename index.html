About

I am an Assistant Professor (Grade-I) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NITK Surathkal, where I joined on 28 October 2026. Before this I was at NIT Srinagar (2021 to 2026), at Madanapalle Institute of Technology and Science (2015 to 2021), at Government College of Technology, Coimbatore as a DST INSPIRE Fellow, and at Karunya as a Lecturer. I completed my Ph.D. at Anna University, Chennai, on personalised information retrieval.

My work sits where model design meets deployment: lightweight deep learning for UAV vision, on-device AI, and deep model compression, alongside a longer-running line in information retrieval and natural language processing. I am interested in how far a network can be shrunk before it stops being useful, and in what that makes possible on a drone or a microcontroller. The applications I return to are environmental monitoring, fetal and maternal health, and language tools for medicine and law.

Research

Lightweight deep learning for UAV vision

Compact segmentation and detection models for drone imagery. The anchor application is mapping floating plastic waste in lakes and coastal waters, supported by the FloPWD and AquaPlasticWaste datasets and the EcoEdgeAI project. A newer thread studies vision-only 360° threat detection and trajectory prediction for drones in physics-based simulation.

On-device (embedded) AI

Running inference directly on drone-mounted and microcontroller-class hardware such as Jetson boards, with no network offloading. The questions are latency, memory and energy budgets, and keeping accuracy usable under them.

Deep model compression

Quantisation, pruning and efficient architecture design, and the trade-offs between size and accuracy. I apply them in vision and in healthcare, for example lightweight and federated models for fetal ultrasound and fetal health assessment.

Information retrieval and natural language processing

My doctoral area: personalised retrieval, term-association graphs and query reformulation. Today it covers summarisation with large language models, retrieval-augmented question answering, legal and biomedical text, and adverse drug reaction signal mining from clinical text.
