AI models small enough to fly on a drone, used on problems that matter close to home.

My group compresses and deploys deep learning models on drone and microcontroller hardware, and applies them to floating plastic in lakes and coasts, fetal ultrasound, and drug safety.

About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NITK Surathkal, where I joined in October 2026. Before this I was at NIT Srinagar (2021 to 2026), at Madanapalle Institute of Technology and Science as Associate Professor and later Professor (2015 to 2021), and at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences. I completed my Ph.D. at Anna University, Chennai, on personalised information retrieval, supported by a DST INSPIRE Fellowship.

My work sits where model design meets deployment. I am interested in how far a deep network can be shrunk through quantisation, pruning and careful architecture choices before it stops being useful, and in what that makes possible on a drone, a microcontroller or a clinic laptop. The applications I return to are environmental monitoring, maternal and fetal health, and the safety of medicines.

Research

Model compression and on-device AI for UAV vision

Quantisation, pruning and lightweight architectures that let segmentation and detection models run directly on drone-mounted hardware, with no network offloading. The anchor application is mapping floating plastic waste in freshwater and coastal waters, including the FloPWD aerial dataset and a lightweight segmentation model built for Jetson-class boards. A newer thread studies vision-only 360° threat detection and trajectory prediction for drones, trained in physics-based simulation.

Language models for domain question answering

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA and QLoRA) of large and small language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and summarisation for biomedical and legal text, including a dataset for legal question answering in the Indian judiciary. The concern throughout is factual reliability and how little compute a useful model needs.

Trustworthy healthcare AI and federated learning

Interpretable, carefully validated and privacy-preserving deep learning for clinical decision support, with fetal ultrasound plane classification and cardiotocography as the main cases. Federated training lets hospitals contribute to a model without sharing patient images.

Graph neural networks and pharmacovigilance

Heterogeneous knowledge graphs built from drug and adverse-event sources, and graph models that predict drug–adverse reaction links and flag safety signals.

Where this began: my doctoral work built semantic term-association graphs, topic models and evolutionary and swarm optimisation into a personalised information retrieval system.
